Museo de la Memoria Colonial

Museum of Colonial Memory

This virtual museum explores Philippine literature during the Spanish colonial era (1565 to 1898). Walk through three salas: Sala I, the first printed books; Sala II, faith and tradition; and Sala III, reform and revolution. At the end, Sala IV tests what you learned with a short quiz.

Drag to turn the camera. W A S D or arrow keys to walk, or use Back and Next. Click a frame to read it. Some frames are songs: open them and press Play. Turn around at the entrance to find the door to the Sala del Teatro, or press Plays room. Walk up to the door and press E to open it.
